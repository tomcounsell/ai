02 · How to read a receipt

A receipt is what GitHub recorded

The receipt line measures the time from the pull request being opened to its merge, and the size of the change in commits, files and lines added and removed. Every figure on it is read from the pull request on GitHub, where anyone can check it. The per-task model spend and the questions asked of Tom sit in the ledger on each Mac and are not yet published here.
