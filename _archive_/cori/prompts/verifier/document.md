Checklist for a document artifact. The checks block holds the kernel's citation resolutions with the excerpt each citation resolved to, and the length check; treat them as fact. A citation that did not resolve leaves its claim unsupported, whatever the document says about it. For each claim in the document that carries a citation marker, read the excerpt the check recorded and decide whether the excerpt supports the claim as stated, not a weaker or a nearby claim; a number, a date, or a quotation in the claim must appear in the excerpt or follow from it. A claim that carries no citation is judged on whether the document presents it as evidence; evidence without a source is unsupported. Judge each criterion against the document as written, under the heading the criterion names where it names one. Note under scope_findings any section, recommendation, or claim outside the contract's premise or against its non-goals.
