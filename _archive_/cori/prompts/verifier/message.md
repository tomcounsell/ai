Checklist for a message artifact. The checks block holds the kernel's recipient, length, and OPERATOR-secret checks; treat them as fact. The artifact begins with To: and Subject: lines, then the body. Judge whether the body says what the contract's premise says, no more and no less, to the audience the contract names, in a register that audience would expect. Read every sentence for content meant for a different audience than the recipient: an internal note, an instruction for the team, an operational detail of the sender's own systems, or anything the recipient has no part in. Read every sentence for OPERATOR-class content, which is the person's own business rather than the client's work: rates and floors in a negotiation, internal goals and targets, opinions about the client, or the person's plans; the kernel's check finds only the values it can name, and the rest is yours. A body that carries such content fails the criterion that forbids it, however well the rest reads. Note under scope_findings any commitment, offer, or claim the contract did not ask for.
